How It Began

Our Love Story

Every great love has a beginning. Here are the unforgettable milestones that paved our path to forever.

Chapter I • Autumn 2021

How We Met

A chance encounter at a cozy bookstore café on a rainy Saturday. What began as asking to share the last table evolved into three hours of effortless conversation, endless coffee, and the discovery of a profound mutual connection.

☕
Chapter II • Spring 2022

The First Date

A golden sunset walk along the riverbanks under glowing trees. We spent hours sharing dreams, childhood stories, and ambitions until the night sky was filled with stars. That evening, we both knew something extraordinary was taking root.

🌅
Chapter III • Winter 2025

The Proposal

Under starlit skies on a quiet mountain getaway, Imanule got down on one knee overlooking the valley. With tears of pure happiness, Kasandra joyfully said “Yes!” to spending forever hand in hand.

💍
Chapter IV • October 19, 2027

Forever Begins

Surrounded by our dearest parents, cherished families, and lifelong friends, we step forward together into our greatest adventure as husband and wife.
